Your CPU, Memory and top-level Computer chips.





Logic highlights


instruction[15]
0 loads a constant into A. 1 is a C-instruction.


instruction[12]
Chooses A or RAM[A] (inM) as the ALU’s second input.


instruction[5:3]
Destination bits: A, D and memory write (writeM).


instruction[2:0]
Jump bits, combined with the ALU’s zr and ng flags.

CPU & Screen Emulator
